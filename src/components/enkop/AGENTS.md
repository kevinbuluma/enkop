# ENKOP UI rules (media, hero, transitions, seasonal atmospheres, brand)

- Property imagery is mapped from local editorial assets by image key; illustrative listings are explicitly labelled until genuine inventory is supplied.
- The homepage hero video files live in public/videos/scenes (MP4, WebM and a poster per scene) because the site is also deployed to Vercel, where Lovable's /__l5e asset URLs return 404; never point hero media at /__l5e or .asset.json URLs.
- Property tour videos are generated server-side via AI Gateway /v1/videos, polled by the admin client, and copied into the private property-media bucket on completion because gateway downloads expire.
- Homepage hero scenes come from the homepage_slides table; the public loader signs only active slide paths server-side. The current hero is a single scene, the ENKOP landing walkthrough (`homepage/enkop-landing.mp4`, served from public/videos/scenes as a seamless loop: enkop-landing-4k.mp4, a sharpened 4K upscale, on screens 1024px and wider, and enkop-landing.mp4, sharpened 1080p, on smaller landscape screens; upright phones and tablets (`PORTRAIT_SCREEN` in src/lib/hero-media.ts) get the separately shot vertical walkthrough enkop-landing-vertical.mp4 with its own poster, while laptops and desktops keep the wide footage; every file must stay under Cloudflare's 25 MiB static-asset limit); it is also the built-in fallback. The earlier three scenes stay in storage and public/ but their slides are inactive.
- Matching homepage fallback images use a browser-captured video frame and an admin-verified streaming AI Gateway image-edit route; only an explicitly approved final image is stored and assigned to the scene.
- Homepage playback uses path-keyed MP4/WebM variants from public/videos/scenes for existing footage, preloads only the current and next scenes, and reveals videos only after playback starts; changed upload paths keep their own footage.
- Built-in footage preserves source texture without denoising, with restrained sharpening and bounded compression: wide screens select upscaled 4K and phones select HD via media-qualified sources, retaining original codec fallbacks and never substituting enhanced built-ins for new uploads.
- Homepage hero is a tour: HeroReel owns its parallax wrapper, a CSS push-in camera per scene and a walk-through leaving layer, with no on-screen controls (removed at the client's request). Because the tour cannot be paused, reduced-motion visitors get the first scene as a still image with no auto-advance.
- Page transitions use a persistent root-level curtain with an in-memory public-navigation counter alternating seasonal effects and page flips; router lifecycle events drive the non-blocking reveal (at most MAX_HOLD_MS, 1.2s, fully covered before revealing; slower loads show the looping gold `html.is-route-loading` bar, and links preload on intent), native snapshot transitions stay disabled, and reduced motion, same-page and admin navigation bypass without advancing the counter.
- Page flips capture an inert, transient outgoing-page DOM image across curved CSS-3D strips (20, or 12 below 760px via `paperStripsFor`), copying only on-screen sections; remove snapshots on completion and keep all snapshot work browser-only to preserve navigation and SSR safety. Per-frame values are set on childless layers (strip shade/light/back, curtain, cast shadow), never as custom properties on ancestors of the page copies, which would restyle every copied element each frame.
- The router swaps pages immediately, so the leaves keep a frozen copy of the outgoing page (`.outgoing-page`, playing videos as their current frame) under them and drop it only at full cover (the reveal); the new page is never visible before the leaves cover the screen. Snapshot copies use `.paper-page-snapshot` instead of `.site-shell`, which page code looks up.
- Autumn navigation uses a preloaded photographic botanical sprite atlas (src/assets/autumn-leaf-cinematic.webp, 1024px, ~190 KB; keep it small so the leaves are ready on the first navigations), cached soft-focus variants and deterministic canvas wind paths; skip the effect if textures are unavailable rather than rendering illustrated substitutes, keeping navigation immediate and per-frame rendering filter-free.
- Rain seasons (`long-rains`, `short-rains`) use the storm atmosphere instead of particles: src/lib/storm (pure, unit-tested models: atmosphere profile, lightning plan, water droplets, rainfall, scene zones, WebGL storm clouds with a 2D fallback, synthesised optional sound) and src/components/enkop/storm. `SeasonalAtmosphereController` is mounted once in the root (never on /admin), plays the cloud entrance once per visit (sessionStorage `enkop:storm-entered`, after the brand intro) and keeps `RainfallBackground` (z 31) and `RainGlassOverlay` (z 32) running across routes, raised to 185/190 while `html[data-page-transition="rain"]`. Every rain-season navigation uses the cloud transition (outgoing page frozen under the clouds until full cover, then the clouds part; never longer than the MAX_HOLD_MS rule). Lightning is a single soft pulse per strike, at least 900ms apart, brightness capped. Droplets refract photos and videos from small cached copies; `SceneZones` keeps text, prices, specs, forms and controls clear (no still water, faint rain). No static rain image, CSS rain loop or full-screen blur. The footer toggle cycles On / Gentle / Off (`data-atmosphere`, localStorage `enkop:season`); reduced motion gets still droplets and a brief opacity veil (`data-mood="calm"`). Sound is off by default, starts only from a visitor gesture, and its toggle (`.rain-sound-toggle`) is always visible while the rain runs. Admins preview with `/?season=<season>` and end it with `/?season=auto` or the preview chip.
- Winter uses the winter environment instead of particles: pure, unit-tested models in src/lib/winter (settings/profile, wind and snowfall, frost dendrites, crack/shard geometry, blizzard drift layout, snowman placement) and browser-painted textures (no image assets), with layers in src/components/enkop/winter. `WinterEnvironmentController` is mounted once in the root (never on /admin), reads the admin's `winter` block inside the `season` site setting (no schema change), and publishes it on <html> (`data-winter`, `-frost`, `-snow`, `-transition`, `-transition-intensity`) for CSS surfaces and `SeasonalPageTransition`, which runs `SnowstormTransition` or `IceTransition` instead of the seasonal/flip alternation. Calm mode and reduced motion get still snow and no winter transition; snowmen only stand in `SNOWMAN_SPOTS` that don't touch text, controls or imagery.
- Identity: royal blue (`--royal` #142B59) establishes the brand and premium gold (`--gold` #C6A45A) refines it, on warm ivory (`--ivory`), midnight (`--midnight`) and soft stone (`--stone`); tokens and their contrast notes are at the top of the ENKOP section of src/styles.css. Gold never carries text or essential icons on light surfaces (2.1:1); use `--gold-ink` there and `--gold`/`--gold-soft` only on royal, midnight or imagery. Selected states and focus are royal on light and soft gold on dark. Buttons use the `Button` variants `primary`, `premium` (viewing/enquiry), `line`, `onDark`, `textArrow` and `iconFrame` (classes `enkop-btn*`, `enkop-text-action`, `enkop-icon-frame`): 2px architectural corners, no pills, metallic gradients or glows. Editorial italics are royal on light and soft gold on dark. Seasons may tint `--background`/`--surface` and set `--season-glow`, but never override `--royal`, `--gold` or `--accent`; season particles fall continuously above page sections (z-index 40, below the header and gallery viewer, pointer-events none) and are cleared only from photos and videos.
- Brand: the ENKOP logo lives in public/brand (original JPG plus transparent cut-outs). Use the BrandLockup (building mark + ENKOP with "Real Estate" in gold beneath it, `--gold` on dark and `--gold-ink` on light headers, auto-switching white/navy on dark/light headers) and BrandLogo (full stacked logo) components from src/components/enkop/BrandLogo.tsx instead of a text wordmark; favicon.ico, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png and brand/enkop-og.jpg are generated from the same artwork, recoloured to the brand royal blue and gold.
- Phone layouts use bounded grid tracks, readable form fields and safe-area-aware scrollable overlays; pointer-driven tilt is reserved for fine hover-capable pointers to keep touch scrolling stable.
